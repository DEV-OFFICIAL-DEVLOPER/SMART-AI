Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SMART AI. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use our AI-powered website.

2. Information We Process

SMART AI may process information including:

  • Messages submitted to AI chat

3. AI Services

AI chat messages and image-generation prompts are sent to the SMART AI backend, which uses Cloudflare services to process requests and generate results.

Do not submit passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information in AI prompts.

4. Local Browser Storage

SMART AI uses browser storage for features such as chat history, theme preferences, and locally saved AI memory.

This information may remain on your device until it is cleared or overwritten.

5. Accounts and Security

SMART AI provides account registration and login features. Account information is processed by the backend.

Security measures are used to protect authentication information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

6. Third-Party Services

SMART AI uses GitHub Pages for website hosting and Cloudflare for backend services and AI processing.

These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Advertising

SMART AI may introduce advertising in the future. If advertising services are enabled, this policy will be updated to describe relevant data processing, cookies, and user choices.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as SMART AI develops new features or changes its services.

9. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning personal information, please use the contact information provided on the SMART AI Contact page once it is available.
